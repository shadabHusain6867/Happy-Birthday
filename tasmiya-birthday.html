a letter, before the rest of this page

I don't really know how to start this, because everything about us started so strangely well. We found each other online on the 3rd of March, and it should have felt like chance. It didn't. Every choice matched — the small things, the big things, even the words we reached for. I don't think I've met anyone who fit so exactly into the shape of what I didn't know I was looking for.

On the 16th of April we made promises to each other, and I meant every one of them. By the 4th of July I couldn't hold it in any longer, so I told you I loved you first — though honestly, some part of me had loved you since March.

Since then, this thing between us has only grown. Not louder, just steadier. Like something that was always going to happen, and finally did.

I love your smile before you even mean to smile. I love your eyes, and the way jealousy shows up in them before you say a word. I love how loyal you are, how possessive, how unapologetically yourself. I love that we are, somehow, so alike.

Happy birthday, Tasmiya. Twelve days from mine to yours, and I still can't believe I get to celebrate you at all.
